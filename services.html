Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers about our services. Still curious? Reach out anytime.

Do I need a label to use Stackly?
Not at all. BeatFlow is built for independent artists. You can upload, distribute, and monetize your music without any label involvement.
How fast do I get paid royalties?
Royalties are calculated daily and paid out automatically to your linked bank account on the 1st of every month.
Can I sell tickets for free events too?
Yes, free RSVP events are supported alongside paid ticketing, with the same QR check-in system.
Is live streaming included in every plan?
Live streaming is available from the Artist Pro plan onward. Listener accounts can watch but not broadcast.
