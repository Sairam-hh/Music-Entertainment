About BeatFlow

Built for Artists. Loved by Fans.

BeatFlow was born out of frustration with fragmented platforms — artists had to manage their music, events, fans, and revenue across five different tools. We built one platform to rule them all.

Today, we power India's most vibrant music community, from emerging bedroom producers in Vizag to headlining artists at NH7 Weekender.

  • Royalty-first model — artists keep 80% of revenue
  • Direct-to-fan ticketing with zero middlemen
  • Real-time analytics dashboard for every artist
  • 24/7 support from a team of music lovers
