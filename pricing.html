Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BeatFlow pricing and plans.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes — Pro Fan comes with a 30-day free trial. No credit card required to start. Artist Pro includes a 14-day free trial for new artists.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, all major credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets like Paytm, PhonePe, and Google Pay. All transactions are secured.

How does artist royalty payout work?

Artists on the Pro plan earn 80% of all streaming revenue. Payouts are calculated monthly and transferred directly to your linked bank account within 5 business days.

Can I use BeatFlow for commercial events?

Absolutely. Our Event Organizer plan (part of Artist Pro) allows you to create and sell tickets for commercial events, festivals, and private performances with full analytics.

Is my data safe on BeatFlow?

Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption, GDPR-compliant data handling, and never sell your personal data to third parties. Your music and financial data are encrypted at rest.
