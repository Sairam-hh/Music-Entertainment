How It Started

One Frustration. One Big Idea.

In 2020, three friends — a musician, a developer, and an event manager — were tired of juggling five different apps just to release a single track and book a single venue. So they built BeatFlow: one platform for streaming, ticketing, royalties, and fan connection.

Today that small idea has grown into India's fastest-rising music and entertainment ecosystem, but the mission hasn't changed — make the business of music disappear so artists can focus on the art.

  • Founded by musicians, for musicians
  • Bootstrapped before our first funding round
  • Profitable royalty-sharing model from day one
  • Headquartered in India, built for global reach

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Two simple ideas guide every decision we make at BeatFlow.

Our Mission

To give every artist — from a bedroom producer in a small town to a stadium-filling headliner — the tools, audience, and fair revenue they deserve, without needing a label or a middleman.

Our Vision

A world where music discovery, live experiences, and artist income all live on one trusted platform — where fans feel closer to artists than ever before, and artists keep what they earn.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

From launch day to 12 million users — here's how we got here.

2020 — The Launch

BeatFlow goes live with streaming and artist profiles for 500 early-access musicians in Andhra Pradesh.

2021 — Ticketing Launches

We introduce direct-to-fan event ticketing, powering our first 100 live shows with zero middlemen.

2022 — 1 Million Users

BeatFlow crosses 1 million monthly active users and opens its first regional office in Hyderabad.

2023 — Live Streaming

Artists can now broadcast performances live with real-time tipping and interactive fan chat.

2025 — National Scale

BeatFlow hosts over 5,000 live events and expands artist tools to cover royalties, merch, and analytics.

2026 — 12M+ and Growing

Today, BeatFlow powers India's largest independent artist community with 12 million+ active users.
