Our Story

Born Backstage

Stackly started in a basement studio in 2010, three friends armed with a mixer, a dream and a stubborn belief that live music should feel like magic.

Fifteen years later we operate across four continents, produce 200+ shows a year and run streaming infrastructure trusted by some of the biggest names in entertainment.

But our DNA hasn't changed — every show is still personal, still crafted, still loud.
